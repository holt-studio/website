Privacy policy

Short version: we only collect what you type into our contact form, we use it only to get back to you about a website, and we never sell it.

Who we are

Holt Design is a small web design business based in Toronto, Ontario. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). If you have a privacy question, email admin@holtdesign.site. That inbox is read by the person responsible for privacy at Holt Design.

What we collect

When you fill in the callback form, we collect:

  • your name
  • your business name
  • your email address
  • your phone number

If you email us, we also have whatever you put in that email.

We don't use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies on this site.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to you: to call or email you about your website, send a quote, and, if you become a client, to run your account. We won't add you to a mailing list or use your details for marketing without asking first.

Consent

By sending the form you agree to us using your details as described here. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing us, and we'll delete your details unless we're legally required to keep them (for example, invoices for tax records).

Who else handles it

We don't sell, rent or trade your information. A few services help run this site:

  • Web3Forms receives the form and forwards it to our email inbox.
  • Our email provider stores the messages we receive.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may log basic technical data such as your IP address for security.
  • Font and code providers (Fontshare, Google Fonts and jsDelivr) serve fonts and scripts to your browser, so they can see your IP address when the page loads.

Some of these providers may store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States. When that happens, the information is subject to the laws of that country.

How long we keep it

If you don't become a client, we delete your form details within 12 months of your last contact with us. If you do, we keep them while you're a client and as long as needed for tax and legal records afterward.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep it safe. Only the people at Holt Design who need your details to reply to you can see them, and the site and form are served over HTTPS.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the personal information we have about you
  • correct it if it's wrong
  • delete it

Email admin@holtdesign.site. We'll reply within 30 days.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
