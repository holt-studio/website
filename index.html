BEFORE YOU ASK

Good questions.

Why is there a 12-month minimum?

There's no setup fee. Instead of a big upfront bill for design and build, that cost is spread across your first 12 months.

What happens after 12 months? Can I cancel?

After your first 12 months you go month-to-month. Cancel any time with 30 days' notice by email. If you cancel before the 12 months are up, the remaining months of that first year are still due.

Who owns my domain and content?

You do. Your domain is registered in your business's name. Your text, photos and logo are always yours. If you leave, we'll help move your domain and send you a copy of your content.

What counts as an "edit"?

One small change, about 30 minutes of work: new text, a swapped photo, updated hours, prices or menu items. New pages, new features or a redesign are bigger jobs, so we'll quote those separately first.

Do unused edits roll over?

No. Each month starts fresh with your plan's edits.

How fast are edits done?

Email your request to admin@holtdesign.site. Edits are done within 2 business days.

How does "live in 7 days" work?

The 7 days start once we have what we need from you: your text, photos and logo, or a quick call so we can write it with you.

What currency are prices in? Is tax included?

All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD) and don't include sales tax. Where HST applies, it's added to your invoice.
